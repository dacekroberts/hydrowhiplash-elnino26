# Addendum 2: pictures of the region map

For the film already in this chat. The site's link card has a finer map than the shapes in the first bundle, with islands kept. These pictures show it whole and in closer views, so the film's map illustrations can follow its look.

## The pictures

| File | Shows |

| `addendum2_map_alaska_dark.png` | Alaska with the Aleutians and Kodiak |

| `addendum2_map_california_dark.png` | the Pacific Northwest coast, NorCal and SoCal |

The first picture is the card's map enlarged 4 times, lines and labels with it. A closer view has its shapes at 4 times and its lines and labels at 2 times, so narrow channels show.

## Rules

- These pictures are a guide to the look of the film's map: the shape of the coast, which islands are drawn and how the lines are weighted. map.json in the first bundle stays the source for where things sit; nothing is traced from a picture into a position.
- Islands stay islands: water between an island and the mainland is drawn as water.
- Two lines. A border between regions is the bright line. A coast is a thin faint line, except British Columbia's, which carries the bright line drawn inside the shore so a narrow channel stays dark.
- The seven regions are the study's regions, filled in one shade. The map shows where they are; it does not show climate zones, and no shading inside a region means anything.
- NorCal and SoCal are too narrow for their names, so each name sits in a pill over the ocean with a line to the region.
- The credit 'Map: Natural Earth' is on screen wherever the map is (credits.md in the first bundle).
- The film stays at 150 s at most. Nothing else in it changes.
