## Findings

### M1 · "CNFDB holds no Parks Canada polygon for 2016-2021" is false as written (medium, misleading record)

- **Evidence.** Own read of the pinned `NFDB_poly.zip`, both shapefiles, `SRC_AGENCY = 'PC'`, `YEAR >= 1984` (`r4_probe.py`, `r4_cnfdb_pc.py`): Parks Canada polygons per year 2015 111, **2016 5, 2017 8, 2018 0, 2019 0, 2020 0, 2021 54**, 2022 66. The 2016 and 2017 polygons are all Nahanni (`NAT_PARK` NA, Northwest Territories; 2017 holds three of 890-1,001 ha), none touching AB, BC or YT. The 2021 set holds 20 small polygons in Alberta parks (Jasper, Banff, Waterton, Elk Island and others; 126 ha in total, largest 65 ha) and 8 in BC (25 ha), none at or above 405 ha; the 9 large 2021 polygons (up to 14,163 ha) are Nahanni again. So the true statements are: Parks Canada sent nothing for **2018-2020** (as the update log says, "Missing years; 2018-2020"), sent Nahanni only for 2016-2017, and did report 2021 ("2016-2017; 2021-2024 updates added in this update"); and **no Parks Canada polygon at or above the floor lies in the study regions in 2016-2021**. `fire_events.csv` agrees: 0 counted Parks Canada pieces in seasons 2016-2021 (16 in 2015, 2 in 2022).
- **Where it is written.** `src/parks_gap.py:3` ("CNFDB holds no Parks Canada polygon for 2016-2021"), `src/config.py:230-231`, `docs/methods_register.md` "Parks Canada gap" (Why column: "CNFDB holds no Parks Canada polygon for 2016-2021 (its update log says 2018-2020)"), `docs/plan_deviations.md` D69 ("CNFDB holds no Parks Canada polygon for 2016-2021"), `docs/data_dictionary.md:499` ("measured 2026-10-08: none for 2016-2021"), `DECISIONS.md` "Audit round 3 settled" ("the gap is 2016-2021 in CNFDB, not 2018-2020 as its update log says; NBAC has no park fire in 2020 or 2021") and the `ANALYSIS_PLAN.md` deviations log, eleventh row ("Parks Canada sent CNFDB no polygons for 2016-2021").
- **What stands.** The flag and the table are right: NBAC shows no park fire at or above the floor in AB, BC or YT in 2020 or 2021, and in 2016-2019 the AB park fires NBAC holds (all Wood Buffalo, plus one Kootenay piece in 2017) have no CNFDB counterpart. The seasons to flag are AB 2016-2019 either way.
- **Suggested fix.** Reword the seven places: "CNFDB holds no Parks Canada polygon at or above 405 ha in the study regions for 2016-2021 (none at all for 2018-2020; Nahanni only in 2016-2017; 2021 reported, small fires only)". The plan's deviations log takes a correcting row, as D70 did; the frozen text is untouched.

### M2 · A second agency gap of the same kind: Department of National Defence lands in Alberta (medium, unexamined)

- **Evidence.** NBAC splits fire events at DND boundaries as it does at park boundaries (`ADMIN_NAME` DND, 102 parts since 1984; `ADMIN_DIV` WW = CFB Wainwright, SF = CFB Suffield in Alberta). Built the same way as the park area (`r4_follow.py` G, `r4_dnd.py`: GID dissolve, prescribed parts dropped, whole fire at or above 405 ha, DND parts clipped to the regions, union per year): DND-land burned area in **AB in 24 of 41 seasons 1984-2024, 109,773 ha in total (0.98% of AB's 11,149,758 counted ha)**; none in BC or YT. **No CNFDB polygon of any agency in any year touches the DND footprint** (0 of the 5,378 CNFDB polygons that touch Alberta 1984-2024), so the omission is structural, not a 2016-2021 reporting gap. Per season (DND ha, counted `burn_ha`, share of the season it would add): 1991 5,264 / 3,593 / 59%; 2020 1,978 / 2,417 / 45%; **2018 10,124 / 42,534 / 19%**; 2013 4,988 / 43,098 / 10%; 2008 2,442 / 22,257 / 10%; 1996 429 / 4,098 / 9.5%; 2001 12,442 / 154,325 / 7.5%; 2005 4,174 / 62,787 / 6%; 2017 2,504 / 60,656 / 4%; 1998 15,755 / 647,914 / 2.4%; the rest 0.1-2.9%. In `log_burn` the largest moves are 1991 +0.90, 2020 +0.60, 2018 +0.21, 2013 +0.11, 2008 +0.10. All 43 AB pieces are MAFiMS (Landsat) mapped and none is flagged prescribed.
- **Why it matters.** D69's reasoning ("the provinces do not map inside the parks") applies to federal training ranges too, and AB 2018, already flagged for its park gap (20% short), is short by about the same again on Wainwright and Suffield. Averaged over the record the omission is about 1% and roughly constant, so its effect on a regression is small; in the four small AB seasons it is not. The register's "Parks Canada gap" row, D69 and the kickoff item frame the agency gap as Parks Canada only. Whether training-range fires count as wildfires is a judgment call the owner has not been asked (NBAC does not flag them prescribed; `FIRECAUS` was not read).
- **Suggested fix.** Record it (register row, data dictionary section 5, a DECISIONS line) as a stated limitation with the per-season shares above; owner's call, before any test and on the same footing as D69, whether the sensitivity table also carries NBAC's DND area for AB (one line in `src/parks_gap.py`: `ADMIN_NAME` in ("PC", "DND"), every AB season, a second column), or stays Parks Canada only.

## Follow-ups for the main session (owner, 2026-10-08: "I would like these addressed")

Beyond M1-M2 and L1-L8, three points sit just outside what this round measured; the owner asked for them to be taken up.

- **F1 · Count the NBAC fires with no CNFDB polygon under them, per Canadian region and season, by NBAC admin label.** Parks Canada (D69) and DND lands (M2) are the two gaps NBAC labels explicitly; the general question is CNFDB completeness, which the deferred NBAC re-run decision already covers (DECISIONS "Audit round 3 settled": 9 AB, 11 BC and 4 YT seasons differ from NBAC by more than 0.25 in `log_burn`). A cheap measurement before that decision: for each NBAC fire at or above 405 ha (GID dissolve, prescribed dropped) in BC, AB and YT, the share of its area with no CNFDB polygon of any agency under it, summed per season and split by `ADMIN_NAME` and `ADMIN_DIV`. - **F2 · AB 2020 is a one-fire season carrying two known omissions.** Counted area 2,417 ha from a single Alberta-agency piece (`fire_events.csv`); the DND gap alone (1,978 ha) would nearly double it, and 2020 is a year Parks Canada sent nothing, so "no park fire in 2020" rests on NBAC alone. - **F3 · The NBAC-added park area is likely a lower bound.** Where both sources map the same Alberta park fires, NBAC runs below CNFDB's agency polygons in most large seasons (ratios 0.68-0.74 in 1998, 2001, 2003, 2004; L1), consistent with Hall et al. 2020 (MAFiMS removes unburned islands and water). - **Process:** keep the measurement script behind the register's Parks Canada numbers in the repository (under `scripts/`, or as functions in `src/parks_gap.py` with a `--measure` flag), so L1 and L2 become one-line checks instead of rebuilds.
