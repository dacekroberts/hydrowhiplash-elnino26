## Findings

### H1 · The CNFDB dissolve merges different fires that share a `FIRE_ID` within a year (high)

- **Evidence.** `src/fire.py:163-189` (`dissolve_cnfdb`) merges every polygon sharing (`fire_id` = agency:FIRE_ID, `cnfdb_year`), with no spatial condition. Own raw read (`r2_dissolve2.py`, all 136 groups since 1984, equal-area CRS): the largest nearest-neighbour gap between a group's parts is 0 km in 25% of groups, 25 km at the median, 327 km at the 75th percentile, 1,109 km at most; 70 of 136 groups have parts more than 5 km apart. Agency ids are plainly reused: Manitoba 2005 ids (`2005336002` etc.) have 3-6 parts scattered 240-830 km apart; Ontario `COC` ids 300-850 km; NWT undated `CAUSE` U ids 190-1,109 km; BC `1985-N30048` 739 km; Alberta `HBZ001` names a different cross-border fire in 2015 and 2023 (the events file holds both, so "47 fires in more than one region" is 48 by (source, id, year)). Of the 13 merged fires among the counted pieces (`fire_events.csv`, `n_parts` = 2), ten have parts touching or within 0.5 km (BC 2003-N40570, BC 2004-N70493, three BC 2006, NT:VQ030-90, NT:EV008-95, NT:SS003-12, NT:FS015-12, PC:2012WB16: a sound merge) and three do not:

  | merged fire | parts | nearest gap | months, causes | effect of the merge |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | BC 1998-N40187 | 349 + 207 ha | 25.1 km | Aug, Aug; H, N | neither part reaches 405 ha; merged 556 ha counted in BC 1998 |
  | BC 1998-N40188 | 283 + 176 ha | 25.9 km | May (H) and Aug (N) | neither reaches 405 ha; merged 459 ha counted in BC 1998, dated 16 May |
  | BC 1994-K50087 | 4,919 + 2,702 ha | 42.8 km | Jul, Jul; H, H | both above the floor already; `n_fires` 11 to 10, area unchanged |

  No 200-350 ha fire has two outlines 25 km apart, and N40188's parts differ in month and cause; these are separate fires that share a number. The register's "CNFDB fires in several polygons" row and DECISIONS "M3 fixed" describe exactly these two 1998 merges as the fix's headline effect ("1998-N40187 556 ha and 1998-N40188 459 ha now pass the floor"; N40188 "kept merged under the rule and flagged to the owner"). Before-and-after from the committed tables (`r2_tables.py`, `git show 6e79757:...`): BC 1998 `burn_ha` 60,120.5 to 61,135.5 (+1,014.9 ha, `log_burn` 11.0041 to 11.0209), `n_fires` 22 to 24; the frozen-rules table the same. The other six changed seasons (BC 2003 +644.7 ha, BC 2006 +32.9 ha, AB 2012 +40.4 ha union, BC 2004, YT 1990, YT 1995 one fire fewer) come from the ten sound merges.
- **Why it matters.** The frozen rule floors "each fire's whole `area_ha`"; a merge of two fires floors their sum. BC 1998 follows the 1997-98 strong El Niño winter, is in a primary region, and enters H2, H3a, H3b, H4, H5, H6, C2 and the frozen-rules run; the error is small (0.017 in `log_burn`, 1.7% of the season's area) but it is a wrong value put into the table by the audit fix, in the direction of the hypothesis. The dissolve's test (`tests/test_fire.py` `test_dissolve_cnfdb_merges_parts_of_one_fire_before_the_floor`) places the two parts of "BC:A" 1 unit apart and never exercises distance.
- **Suggested fix.** Add a spatial condition to the dissolve: merge only parts that touch or lie within a small distance of another part of the group (connected components at, say, 1 km in ESRI:102008, a value in `config.py`), leaving the rest as separate fires with the id suffixed; log before-and-after (expected: BC 1998 back to 60,120.5 ha and 22 fires, BC 1994 back to 11 fires, every other season unchanged, 136 groups to about 66 merged), add a test with far-apart parts, and re-pin. Alternatively revert the dissolve and record the per-polygon rule with its measured effect (round 1's option b). Either way the register's row, DECISIONS "M3 fixed", `docs/data_dictionary.md` section 5 and the undated-fires row need the new numbers.

### M1 · The plain OLS size check is kept for the H4 contrast although its own size is 0.6%, far from the owner's "near 5%" condition, and it has half the power (medium)

- **Evidence.** D66 and DECISIONS "Audit round 1 settled": the plain OLS p is kept "only where its own measured size is near 5%", and lists the H4 contrast with "0.6% with correlated regions". Own simulations (`r2_sim.py`, `r2_power.py`, errors AR(1) 0.3, regions correlated 0.5 as the audit's realistic case): plain OLS contrast size 0.007 (0.000 at correlation 0.8, 0.045 at 0), Driscoll-Kraay 0.078; power at a true north-minus-SoCal slope difference of 0.10 / 0.15 / 0.20: Driscoll-Kraay 0.38 / 0.64 / 0.88, plain OLS 0.11 / 0.30 / 0.58 (600 datasets each). The reason is structural: the common year shock cancels in a between-region contrast, so plain OLS, which ignores the correlation, overstates the contrast's variance; it is not a size check of the registered test but a different, much weaker test.
- **Why it matters.** Printed beside the registered p, a non-significant plain OLS p for the H4 contrast would read as "the result does not survive the check" when the check could not have found an effect of the size the plan expects. The same number does serve the per-region ENSO tests (H3a, H3b, C1, C2: size 0.04-0.06 here, 0.034 for the C1 interaction).
- **Suggested fix.** Drop the plain OLS check for the H4 contrast (keep it for H3a, H3b, C1, C2), or report it with its size and power stated beside it; amend D66, the plan's deviations row and the register row accordingly. No registered quantity changes.
